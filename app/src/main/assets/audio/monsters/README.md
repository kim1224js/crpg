# Monster sounds

The active files in this directory provide separate attack, hit, and death sounds for spider, bandit, wild dog, and slime. Files under `library/` are reserved for future monsters and are not loaded at runtime.

- Source: Ogrebane, Monster Sound Pack, Volume 1 — https://opengameart.org/content/monster-sound-pack-volume-1
- Source: Ogrebane, Monster Sound Effects Pack — https://opengameart.org/content/monster-sound-effects-pack
- License: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication)
- Changes: files were selected and renamed for their in-game roles; audio content is otherwise unchanged.
