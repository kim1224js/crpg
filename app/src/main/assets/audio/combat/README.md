# Combat sound effects

Source: Kenney Impact Sounds 1.0
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal.
See `KENNEY_LICENSE.txt` in this directory.

Suggested use:

- `weapon_hit_metal_light_*`: sword, spear and light armor hits
- `weapon_hit_metal_medium_*`: strong weapon and shield impacts
- `armor_hit_heavy_*`: heavy armor impacts
- `monster_hit_light_*`: spider and slime hits
- `monster_hit_medium_*`: bandit and wild dog hits
- `monster_hit_heavy_*`: critical or heavy monster hits
- `body_fall_heavy_*`: player or monster death/fall
