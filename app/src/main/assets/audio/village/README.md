# Village sound effects

- `wooden_door_open.wav`: **Door Open SFX** by Oiboo
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/door-open-sfx
- License: CC0 1.0
- Usage: played when entering any village building or the dungeon entrance.

- `door_creak_loud.ogg`: door creak from **100 CC0 SFX** by rubberduck
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx
- License: CC0 1.0
- Usage: louder replacement played when entering a village building or the dungeon entrance.

- `purchase_coins.wav`: **Purchasing Sound Effect** by Spring Spring
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/purchasing-sound-effect
- License: CC0 1.0
- Usage: played once after a paid shop purchase succeeds.
